# ELYNDRAN — Prompts de producción · masters prioritarios v0.9

Estos prompts traducen `ILLUSTRATION_MANIFEST_V09.md` a briefs reproducibles. **No son assets finales**. Un raster solo asciende a master tras revisión visual y comprobación de continuidad.

## Negativos globales

Aplicar a todas las escenas: no extra humans, no open helmet outdoors, no fantasy armor, no medieval aesthetic, no runes, no readable generated text, no HUD, no neon sci-fi city, no giant monster, no magic glow, no visible Aster, no complete platform, no upper platform surface, no invented doors/windows, no heroic poster pose, no oversaturated bioluminescence.

Canon humano: un único protagonista, traje EVA blanco/gris usado, juntas oscuras, mochila rectangular; proporciones humanas realistas; manos anatómicamente correctas. Canon ambiental: raíces suspendidas enormes, humedad, niebla volumétrica natural, profundidad vertical, materiales físicamente plausibles, luz cinematográfica sobria, fotografía de ciencia ficción adulta.

## X-A — Cara inferior de plataforma

**Función:** fijar el lugar que XXIV debe repetir exactamente.

Prompt base:

> Cinematic photorealistic alien expedition photograph from a lower wet root beneath a partially observed regular structure. One small sealed astronaut in a worn white-gray EVA suit stands safely on the living root, not climbing. Ahead and above, show ONLY the underside of an ambiguous dark layered platform-like formation: partial layers, structural ribs, cavities and a living root passing through or wrapping around it. A thick nearby fiber hangs between root and underside but is clearly not a safe bridge. Wet organic bark, thin mist, restrained natural light, huge vertical depth. The relationship between biological root and regular dark material must remain unresolved: neither clearly built nor clearly grown. 35mm cinematic realism, physically plausible materials, subtle scale, no spectacle.

Bloqueos: no superficie superior; no vista completa; no puerta/ventana; no escritura; no protagonista colgado; no anclaje inventado.

## XIV-A — Bóveda + partículas · benchmark 10/10

> Photorealistic cinematic expedition scene inside a dense humid alien canopy formed by suspended roots and layered vegetation. One sealed astronaut in the canonical worn white-gray EVA suit moves carefully through a narrow natural passage. A restrained lateral beam of daylight crosses the scene and reveals ordinary-looking suspended particulate in the air as tiny dust/fiber specks, not glowing spores. The helmet remains fully closed. Surfaces are damp, boots and suit slightly dirty, vegetation physically reacts to humidity and airflow. The image should make filtered breathing feel necessary without showing toxic horror. Documentary realism, shallow atmospheric depth, realistic exposure, premium science-fiction film still.

Bloqueos: partículas no deben parecer esporas mágicas; nada de máscara abierta; nada de laboratorio exterior.

## XV-A — Interior de cápsula / prefiltro

> Tight photorealistic interior of a small damaged descent capsule being used as a survival refuge. The same astronaut is now inside, helmet removed only because the cabin is sealed, wearing the same worn suit base layer/partial EVA gear. Show a physically credible ventilation grille and replaceable prefilter with ambiguous gray mineral/fiber contamination, not biological horror. A simple strip of tape on the floor separates the dirty entry side from the cleaner working side; it is organization, not an airlock. Repairs, condensation marks, reused bags, tools and limited supplies make the capsule feel cramped and vulnerable. Warm practical cabin light, cinematic realism, no futuristic luxury, no giant laboratory.

## XVI-A — Exposición dérmica

> Exterior immediately beside the same damaged capsule on a broad wet suspended root. One astronaut, helmet fully closed, canonical worn white-gray EVA suit intact except ONLY the back of the LEFT hand and left wrist deliberately exposed for a controlled short skin test. The bare skin does not touch root, plants or water. The right hand remains fully gloved. The astronaut stands close enough to retreat into the capsule immediately, posture cautious and procedural rather than heroic. Natural mist, subdued alien vegetation, realistic anatomy, realistic glove/sleeve transition, cinematic documentary photography.

Bloqueos absolutos: solo mano/muñeca izquierdas; no antebrazo amplio; no mano derecha desnuda; no contacto con flora; no reacción visible sobrenatural.

## Prólogo-A — Descenso

> Wide cinematic photorealistic science-fiction view of a very small descent capsule falling through broken cloud into an alien world dominated by an immense suspended network of living roots at multiple vertical levels. No visible ground. The capsule is utilitarian and vulnerable, not a sleek starship. Mist fills the gaps, water falls from distant root masses, natural storm light reveals scale without fantasy colors. No other spacecraft or humans visible. The image should communicate isolation, uncontrolled descent and the first impossible glimpse of Elyndran without explaining it.

## I-A — Primer paso

> One tiny sealed astronaut standing for the first time on a huge wet suspended root beside a damaged descent capsule lodged behind him. The route ahead curves into mist among roots at different heights. Transparent membrane-like growths cling to nearby bark and react subtly to airflow. The astronaut's body language is careful because of injury, not triumphant. No ground visible, restrained overcast light, realistic wet materials, cinematic photorealism, sense of navigable space and enormous unknown depth.

## I-I1 — Primer mirador

> Same astronaut and same suit at a recognizable natural overlook on the first route. The foreground root is broad enough to stand safely; beyond it, layers of suspended roots vanish into mist. Far away, three extremely small brief specular points appear in slightly descending positions with no visible source. They must look ambiguous and physically plausible, not like beacons or artificial lights. The protagonist remains tiny in frame. No cascade source revealed yet.

## II-A — Nivel inferior y discos

> Photorealistic descent into a warmer, wetter lower layer of suspended roots. The sealed astronaut carefully uses thick natural fibers to reach a lower root where several shallow concave organic disks hold local water. Small flat gray-blue aquatic animals are only partly visible within one disk, ecological and non-threatening. Humidity is visibly higher than above; light is softer through mist. No glowing fantasy pool, no monster, no drinking.

## IV-A — Tres brazos

> Same sealed astronaut observing an ambiguous approximately 2.5-meter formation embedded in a living root. Exactly three curved arms emerge from or are embraced by bark. Interior cavities contain thin transparent films; the structure has regular physical indentations but no readable symbols. A partial distant regular platform may be visible only as an unresolved background geometry through mist. Photorealistic material ambiguity: biological, mineral or manufactured origin cannot be decided from the image.

## V-A — Bóveda nueva

> One sealed astronaut entering a denser ascending micro-biome inside a natural vault of roots and layered alien vegetation. White filament-like growths bend away from the astronaut's lamp, coppery overlapping plates occur farther along the route, and humidity beads on surfaces. The composition should clearly feel different from the first route while remaining part of the same planet. No monster, no glowing jungle, no architecture. Cinematic photorealism, restrained color, real moisture and scale.

## XXV-A — Cascada desde el primer mirador

> Return to the EXACT visual geography of I-I1. Same overlook, same root geometry, same astronaut and suit, now more worn. Far across the suspended root landscape, a distant waterfall catches a moving band of natural light and produces a brief specular reflection compatible with the old three flashes. The cascade remains very distant and no route to it is obvious. The image must feel like reinterpretation of a known place, not revelation of a signal or civilization.

## XXV-I2 — Región de cascadas + planeadores

> Extremely wide photorealistic alien landscape from a high suspended root above a stable bank of mist. The astronaut is tiny. Ahead lies a newly discovered region of roots at many vertical levels with at least seven naturally irregular waterfalls visible across the scene. One side contains a dark mass that resolves as many intertwined roots, clearly natural. One or two living gliding forms around 1.5 meters across use rising air near waterfalls; dark upper surfaces, pale edges, two fine trailing/steering extensions, flexible bodies, no conventional wing flapping. They stay far from the astronaut. No city, no ruins, no towers, no ground, no artificial lights. The scene must radically expand the perceived scale of Elyndran while preserving uncertainty.

## Gate de aprobación

Antes de versionar un raster como master:

1. comparar protagonista/traje/cápsula con masters ya aprobados;
2. comprobar anatomía y escala;
3. verificar que la imagen no revela más que el capítulo;
4. revisar regresos I↔XXV, IV↔XVII y X↔XXIV;
5. rechazar texto generado incrustado;
6. rechazar cualquier elemento que convierta hipótesis en hecho;
7. exigir calidad fotográfica 10/10: iluminación, materiales, profundidad, ausencia de artefactos y composición editorial útil en 6×9.

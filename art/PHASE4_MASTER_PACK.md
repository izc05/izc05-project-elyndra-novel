## X-A.webp — Capítulo X · Una hora más

### Escena
Aster, completamente sellado en EVA Mark-I, visto desde detrás y ligeramente por debajo bajo una raíz principal húmeda. Mira hacia la cara inferior parcial de una plataforma apenas visible entre niebla, corteza y crecimiento vivo. Solo aparecen bandas oscuras locales, dos o tres nervaduras y surcos transversales. La geometría global debe seguir siendo imposible de reconstruir.

### Prompt maestro
Photorealistic cinematic science-fiction expedition still on the alien world Elyndran. One lone human explorer, Aster, wearing the exact same used graphite-grey EVA Mark-I exploration suit, closed helmet, compact rectangular life-support backpack, standing beneath a massive wet living root in dense cold mist. Camera behind and slightly below the explorer, looking upward. Only a PARTIAL underside of an unknown embedded structure can be seen through fog and bark: a dark half-meter-wide band with regular transverse grooves, two or three exposed ribs disappearing under organic bark, wet roots growing around and over the material. The object must remain ambiguous and incomplete, physically ancient because it is integrated with living growth. Strong vertical depth, humid atmosphere, realistic wet materials, subtle flashlight, restrained natural colors, documentary expedition photography, cinematic realism, physically plausible scale. Do not show the upper surface, complete outline, aerial view, doors, symbols, circuits, windows, lights, screens, runes, recognizable spacecraft, bridge or building. No second human, no open helmet, no embedded text, no HUD, no fantasy concept-art look.

### Ancla narrativa
Insertar después de que el texto haya establecido que Aster observa la cara inferior y puede distinguir regularidad local, pero antes de cualquier deducción posterior que permita interpretar función u origen.

## XIV-A.webp — Capítulo XIV · El aire que gastas

### Escena
Aster sellado en la bóveda ascendente. Un haz de linterna cruza lateralmente filamentos blancos y hace visibles miles de partículas suspendidas. El objetivo es mostrar materia en el aire, no explicar su naturaleza.

### Prompt maestro
Photorealistic cinematic expedition still on Elyndran, continuing the exact same Aster and EVA Mark-I visual identity. A lone explorer in a used graphite-grey EVA exploration suit with closed helmet and installed breathing filter stands small inside a vast humid ascending vault. Camera slightly behind the shoulder at medium-long distance. A narrow flashlight beam cuts sideways through pale white filaments and reveals thousands of tiny suspended particles ONLY inside the illuminated volume; outside the beam they disappear into shadow and mist. Wet mineral-organic walls, restrained copper plates and subtle green columns, cold damp atmosphere, realistic condensation, enormous environmental scale, quiet scientific tension. The particles must remain visually ambiguous: no readable spores, no giant pollen, no microorganisms, no swarm shapes. No toxic green smoke, no open helmet, no skin, no monsters, no dramatic warning HUD, no text, no fantasy glow. Cinematic photorealism, physically plausible optics and atmospheric scattering.

### Ancla narrativa
Colocar cuando el manuscrito ya ha descrito la visibilidad de partículas en el haz y antes de que cualquier análisis posterior pueda sugerir qué son.

### QA obligatorio

- [ ] casco cerrado;

Interior íntimo de la cápsula. Aster está sentado en el suelo con la pierna derecha extendida. La rejilla de retorno está abierta a la altura del tobillo y el prefiltro descansa sobre una bandeja. La línea de cinta separa de forma improvisada zona de entrada y zona limpia.

### Prompt maestro
Photorealistic cinematic interior of the same damaged Elyndran descent capsule used throughout the story. Tight, practical, repaired spacecraft interior, not a laboratory. Aster sits on the floor with the right leg extended and unloaded, helmet removed only because the return procedure is complete. The return-air grille is open near ankle height. A folded used prefilter lies on a small metal tray beside screws and a flashlight. Fine dust, medical-bag textile fibers and a few ambiguous translucent insulation fragments are visible. A short strip of tape across less than one meter of floor marks an improvised boundary between dirty entry side and cleaner rest/analysis side; it must look fragile and practical, not sealed. Cold natural light from hatch/window with very restrained warm functional light. Same capsule geometry, same accumulated damage and repairs, intimate scale, grounded science-fiction realism. No mold on walls, no floating spores, no infection, wounds or body horror, no sterile large lab, no futuristic invented interfaces, no airtight decontamination chamber, no embedded text.

### Prompt maestro
Photorealistic cinematic controlled exposure test immediately outside the same Elyndran capsule. One lone explorer, Aster, seated or half-seated on a broad relatively dry section of alien root with back supported by a natural protrusion, right leg extended, the full nearby capsule hatch clearly visible within a few meters. Aster wears the exact same used graphite-grey EVA Mark-I suit, closed helmet and active breathing system. ONLY the back of the LEFT hand and wrist are exposed to the cold humid air; left glove partially removed, right glove still fully worn, every other part of the body sealed. The exposed hand must be anatomically flawless, natural, small within the composition, not touching the root. A discreet physical timing device may be present without readable numerals dominating the image. Nearby membranes are slack. Subtle cold moisture on skin and restrained natural light, quiet controlled scientific risk, no hero pose. No open helmet, no face exposure, no bare arm or torso, no skin touching alien material, no rash, glowing veins, adaptation, organisms approaching, fantasy effects, HUD, or embedded text.
